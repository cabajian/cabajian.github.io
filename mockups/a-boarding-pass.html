Getting here

Travel & Stay

Everything happens within a few blocks of the Old Port.

By air

Portland International Jetport (PWM) is about 10 minutes from the Old Port.

By rail

The Amtrak Downeaster runs from Boston North Station to Portland.

By car

Take I-295 into Portland. Garages along Commercial Street fill up on summer weekends.

Good to know

Ask us Anything

What should I wear?

Coastal cocktail: linen, sundresses, light suits. Flat or wedge shoes for the deck, and a layer, since the bay can be breezy even in June.

Is everyone on the boat?

Yes! Plan to be at the pier 20 minutes before boarding. Details on the exact berth will follow with your invitation.

What happens if it rains?

We'll share our weather plan here closer to the day.

Can I bring a guest?

Your invitation will list everyone we've saved a seat for.
