Questions, Answered

What should I wear?

Coastal cocktail: linen, sundresses, light suits. Flat shoes for the deck, and a layer for the breeze.

Is everyone on the boat?

Yes! Plan to be at the pier twenty minutes before boarding.

What if it rains?

We'll share our weather plan here closer to the day.

Can I bring a guest?

Your invitation will list everyone we've saved a seat for.

Where do I park?

Garages along Commercial Street are closest. Summer weekends are busy, so leave extra time.
