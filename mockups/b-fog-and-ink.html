Chapter II

Travel & stay

Everything happens within a few blocks of the Old Port, so once you're here you can walk.

Flying

Portland International Jetport (PWM) is about ten minutes away. Boston Logan is two hours south.

By train or bus

The Amtrak Downeaster and Concord Coach Lines both run from Boston to Portland.

Driving

Take I-295 into the city. Garages along Commercial Street fill up on summer weekends, so leave a little extra time.
